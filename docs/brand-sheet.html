Logo

The logo

A script “be” with a long swash over widely spaced MAJOR. Use it in one solid color: Ink on light backgrounds, white on Be Major Blue or Ink.

Ink on white
White on Be Major Blue
White on Ink
  • Always the version without “Foundation”.
  • Don’t recolor it in coral, teal or periwinkle, stretch it, outline it or add effects.
  • Leave clear space around it at least the height of the “M” in MAJOR.
  • Smallest size: about 96px wide on screen, so the swash stays visible.

Contrast

Text on color

These pairings keep every word readable (WCAG AA). Ratios are text against the background.

InkInkInk

Use: Ink on coral, teal and periwinkle, at any size. About 7:1.

WhiteWhiteWhite

Avoid: white text on coral, teal or periwinkle. About 2.5:1, too faint at any size.

LargeLarge

Use with care: on Be Major Blue, white or Ink text only at 24px and up (about 4:1). For smaller text on blue, switch the background to Blue Deep.

LinkLink

Use: Blue Deep for links and small blue text on white (5.8:1). Avoid: Be Major Blue for small text (4:1).
